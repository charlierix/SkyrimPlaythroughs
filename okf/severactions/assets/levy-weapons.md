# Overview

18 binary art files make up the Levy's signature blades: the General's writ-blade in one- and two-handed meshes, and the Legates' ember and storm blades. Credits (README/info.xml): the writ-blade mesh and textures by **InsanitySorrow** (Insanity's Ebony Sword Replacer, Nexus #37645), recoloured to the Treasury livery; the Legates' blades by **billyro** (Mage Glass Sword, Nexus #38798), re-tinted storm-blue for Livia and ember for Drusilla ([/prompts/enterprises.md](/prompts/enterprises.md)). 18 files, all named below.
