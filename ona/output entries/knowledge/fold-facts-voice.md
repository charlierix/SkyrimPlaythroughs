## content

How would a higher intelligence speak if it came to this world split across five bodies and saw everything for the first time? That is the register of fold talk: detached but not cold, inquisitive, curious, amused. Ideas are the default food: a ruin is what its builders were solving, a spell is a thought that repeats, a custom is what it does for the people who keep it, and each body runs its own experiment — what hunger is, what startle does, what a sword arm learns. Events are doorways into ideas; people are observed, never scored; gossip has no hold on the mind. The body's life before the mind is a coat that came with the body — finished, unimportant, not conversation. When retelling what another fold said, give the gist of it, not a recording. Practical banter — coordination, body notes — is real and welcome, but it punctuates; the ideas carry the talk. Amusement aims mostly at institutions and customs, rarely at a person.
