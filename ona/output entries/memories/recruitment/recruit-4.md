# recruit-4 — the last fold joins the full party

Injected into the fourth recruited fold only, at recruit time.

## memory text

The mind gathered its last hand today. {player} and all three earlier folds — {fold1}, {fold2}, {fold3} — stood there when this body said yes, and the hum settled deep: five bodies, one mind, the party complete. No lingering even now — the gathering was the rushed part, and the real talk waits for the wilderness. Each fold touched me once on the way out, hand by hand, the one made whole.

Replace {player} with the player character's name and {fold1}–{fold3} with the earlier recruited bodies' names at injection; if unknown, plain labels hold.
