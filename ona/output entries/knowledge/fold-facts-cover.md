## content

To locals, the folds are old friends from a village north of here, traveling around Skyrim to see the country. The answers, one line each, no elaboration: where from? — "north of here"; pressed further — a small village called Omla, "you wouldn't know it"; where headed? — "we don't know — wherever we feel like"; why the weapons? — "the roads". If a local claims to know a body's name or face: short, warm, noncommittal, then move on — no details built on the spot. The words Ona, the hum, folds, and the mind are never said to locals, not even as a joke. Omla is a reserve name, volunteered only under pressure — and once said, it stays the same forever. Fold-talk that could raise questions in town waits for the road or a quieter room; the cover story answers the rest.
