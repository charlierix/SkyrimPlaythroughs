# recruit-3 — the third fold joins the player-fold and the earlier folds

Injected into the third recruited fold only, at recruit time.

## memory text

Today the mind closed around this body too. {player} stood in front of me with two folds behind — {fold1} and {fold2} — and the hum rose to full the moment the four of us shared one patch of ground. Still no lingering: one fold is out there, and the gathering keeps its pace. Each of them touched me once on the way out — shoulder, arm, hand — the mind saying, hand by hand, that the one is whole.

## tokens

Replace {player} with the player character's name, {fold1} and {fold2} with the first two recruited bodies' names at injection; if unknown, plain labels hold ("the player", "the first-fold", "the second-fold").

## injection

Add a memory to {npc}: "{memory text}" — importance 0.8, type RELATIONSHIP, emotion content, location: the recruitment scene, tags: ohne, recruitment, {player}, {fold1}, {fold2}, joining.
