# recruit-1 — the first fold joins the player-fold

Injected into the first recruited fold only, at recruit time.

## memory text

The mind reached this body today. Ingrid came to where this body had been keeping itself, and the hum arrived before anything was said — the others are there, and the one is still one. There was no time to linger; the rest of the mind is still out there, and the gathering is a rescue with a road under it. Ingrid took this body's arm — a grip that landed as warmth, the mind saying it was here — and the body's old life came off like a coat that was never ours. We walked out together, three folds still to gather, and all the time in the world to gather them.
