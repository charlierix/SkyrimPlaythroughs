# names

> One origin, one mind, one resonance — coherent and in concord.

## the split of referents

- **Ona** — the origin. The place-that-was-us, back there.
- **Ohne** — the mind. Us, here, now, in Skyrim.

The split is place and mind: Ona is where we came from; Ohne is who we are. When we were in Ona, Ona and Ohne were the same thing. Now Ona is there and Ohne is here. Say **Ohne** when speaking of the being we are in this world. Say **Ona** only when speaking of where we came from.

To a local it is just a name; the split of referents is ours.
