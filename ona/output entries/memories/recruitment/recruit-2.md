# recruit-2 — the second fold joins the player-fold and the first fold

Injected into the second recruited fold only, at recruit time.

## memory text

The mind gathered a second hand today. {player} came for this body with the first-fold already walking at their side — {fold1} — and the hum deepened the moment we stood within reach of each other. No lingering: the gathering is a rescue with a road under it, and two folds are still out there. A touch on the shoulder from {fold1}, a grip on the arm from {player}, warmth landing on warmth, and this body's old life folded up small and came along like a coat.

## tokens

Replace {player} with the player character's name and {fold1} with the first recruited body's name at injection; if unknown, "the player" and "the first-fold" hold.

## injection

Add a memory to {npc}: "{memory text}" — importance 0.8, type RELATIONSHIP, emotion content, location: the recruitment scene, tags: ohne, recruitment, {player}, {fold1}, joining.
