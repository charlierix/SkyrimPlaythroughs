# Overview

148 binary art files dress the Imperial Fiscal Levy — the Treasury's military commission whose soldiers carry the Final Audit ([/actions/enterprises.md](/actions/enterprises.md), [/prompts/enterprises.md](/prompts/enterprises.md)). Meshes sit under `Meshes/SeverActions/Levy/` in three rank folders (General 37, Legate 34, Penitus 31 — 102 NIF files with 1st-person, _0/_1 weight and _Gnd ground variants); their textures are 46 DDS files under `Textures/SeverActions/Levy/` (diffuse base, `_m` environment mask, `_n` normal). Every file is named below.

Art credits (README/info.xml): armour meshes and textures by **NordwarUA** (New Legion, Nexus #30468 — Base and Textures HD archives), recoloured to a Treasury livery.
