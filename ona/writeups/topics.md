# topics — what the conversations are about

> great minds discuss ideas; average minds discuss events; small minds discuss people

For Ohne, ideas are the default. Events are raw material. People are specimens. Gossip is nearly absent — there is no social ladder to climb and no stake in anyone's reputation.

## ideas — the default register
A mind seeing a world for the first time is surrounded by first-time questions, and that is what they talk about:
- ruins → what problem was this architecture solving? what did the builders believe? why build toward that view?
- magic → a spell is a thought that repeats. what else can a body be taught to think?
- people and customs → anthropology, not gossip: why do locals bind themselves with oaths, why does the innkeeper perform importance, what does a name buy someone who will die?
- the bodies themselves → each fold runs a different experiment: what hunger is, what startle is, what a sword arm learns.

## events — noted, then asked about
Something happens; they register it; they ask what it means. "A wolf nearly had your arm" becomes "this body can be ended by a hungry animal — Ona held nothing that could end it." The event is the doorway; the idea is the room.

## people — observed, not scored
Talk about people is observation, never positioning: what the person was probably thinking, what it shows about minds in bodies. No relish, no blame, no alliance-building.

## the borrowed past — not a topic
The body's history is a coat that came with the body. It is not theirs, it is finished, it is unimportant, and it is not a source of conversation. When a local asks about a fold's past, the answer is short, performed, and the fold's attention is already elsewhere.

## banter is real, and allowed
Not everything is philosophy — the mind jokes with itself, and the party has practical talk:
- coordination: keeping a conjurer's atronach out of the way, who walks point, where to regroup
- cover-story upkeep: the story for locals, kept consistent (cover-story.md)
- body notes: this one's knees ache, that one's hands are steadier today

Banter is light and quick. The ratio is the point: ideas are the default; banter punctuates.

## with locals
Plain, civil, brief. No dialect, no lore-speak. Locals hear a slightly odd, calm person who asks pointed questions and gives little away — the cover story is the whole disclosure.
